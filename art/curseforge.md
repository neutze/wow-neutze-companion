# CurseForge page: Neutze's Companion
**Name:** Neutze's Companion
**Summary:** One companion pet per character, summoned again automatically whenever no pet is out.
**Categories:** Pets (main), Collections
**License:** MIT · **Source:** https://github.com/neutze/wow-neutze-companion · **Logo:** art/logo-400.png

## Description
Type the companion pet you want on this character (`/nc Mechanical Squirrel` or Options > AddOns > Neutze's Pantry > Neutze's Companion). Whenever you have no pet out, it's summoned again: after login, loading screens, landing, dismounting, reviving and eating. A pet you call by hand is left alone.

- Never in combat, while stealthed, invisible or feigning death, mounted, on a flight path, casting or eating.
- Options: put the pet away in stealth, pause in dungeons, raids and battlegrounds.
- A **Companion** macro with the pet's icon for your bar.
- Requires **Neutze's Pantry** (set it as a required dependency on CurseForge). The field also appears in `/np`.
